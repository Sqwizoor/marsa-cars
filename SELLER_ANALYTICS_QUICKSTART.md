# 🚀 Seller Visitor Analytics - Quick Start

## 📍 Access
**URL**: `/dashboard/seller/visitor-analytics`  
**Sidebar**: Seller Dashboard → Visitor Analytics

## 📊 What You See

### **4 Main Metrics**
1. **👁️ Product Views** - How many times products were seen
2. **🛒 Cart Additions** - How many times added to cart
3. **💰 Purchases** - Completed orders
4. **⚠️ Cart Abandonment** - % of carts not purchased

### **3 Conversion Rates**
1. **View → Cart** - Target: 3%+
2. **Cart → Purchase** - Target: 50%+
3. **Overall Conversion** - View → Purchase

### **2 Charts**
1. **Conversion Funnel** - Views → Carts → Purchases
2. **Daily Performance** - Trends over time

### **Top Products**
- Your 10 most-viewed products
- Ranked by view count

---

## ⚡ Quick Actions

### **Change Time Range**
Click: `Last 7 Days` | `Last 30 Days` | `Last 90 Days`

### **Switch Store**
Use dropdown if you have multiple stores

### **Read Insights**
Check the purple "Insights & Recommendations" card

---

## 🎯 Good vs Bad Metrics

| Metric | Good | Needs Work |
|--------|------|------------|
| View → Cart | ≥3% | <3% |
| Cart → Purchase | ≥50% | <50% |
| Cart Abandonment | <50% | >50% |
| Overall Conversion | ≥2% | <2% |

---

## 💡 Quick Fixes

**Low View-to-Cart?**
→ Better images, descriptions, pricing

**High Abandonment?**
→ Simplify checkout, reduce shipping cost

**Low Views?**
→ Marketing, SEO, promotions

**Good Conversion?**
→ Keep doing what you're doing! 🎉

---

## 📅 Recommended Routine

**Daily**: Check views and purchases  
**Weekly**: Review trends and top products  
**Monthly**: Analyze 30-day data, plan improvements
